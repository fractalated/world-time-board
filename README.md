# World Time Board

A single-page world clock: your local time and UTC at the top, and a grid of twelve time zones below (Hawaii, Alaska, Pacific, Arizona, Mountain, Central, Eastern, London, Vietnam, Hong Kong, Japan, and eastern Australia).

Everything lives in `index.html`. Times come from the viewer's device clock and update every second. Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour time with the toggle at the top.
